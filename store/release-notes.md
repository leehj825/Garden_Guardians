# Release notes

The version code Google Play compares comes from the release workflow's run number (see `.github/workflows/release-build.yml`); the version shown to players is `ApplicationDisplayVersion` in `GardenGuardians.csproj`.

## 0.2.0

### Play Console "What's new" (500 characters at most)

```
Bramblekin grow! New skills (fighting, gathering, crafting) and masters wear a gold mark. Mark favourites and get news of their lives. The garden catches up while you were away. Explore: run from the Wolf Spider, run errands for clanmates, find the ant hill and shrines, and talk to anyone. Spring planting days, puddles and falling leaves, stories of friends and rivals, memorials, achievements, new-garden scenarios, and Low detail and Sleep modes.
```

### Full list of changes

**Growth and skills**
*   Three new skills: Fighting (blows dealt to or taken from other Bramblekin; masters hit harder, take less, and look up to 12% bigger), Gathering (picking up food and filling bottles; faster foraging and filling) and Crafting (the clan's best hand makes cloth and cut stone, faster).
*   Skill gains pop up over the Bramblekin you are watching or playing; others show when a skill is first learned, practised or skilled.
*   Masters of any skill wear a small gold mark.

**Following the story**
*   Favourites: mark a Bramblekin in the Kin Inspector and get a banner when it has a child, falls ill, leads, becomes a master or dies.
*   While you were away: a garden opened after a break catches up (up to 10 minutes of garden time; tap to skip) and banners say what happened.
*   Story of the Day: after a busy day, the biggest headline comes round again at dawn; tap it to fly there.
*   Friendship and rivalry stories for leaders, masters and favourites.
*   Memorial stones where Leaders and masters fell, and a Hall of ancestors on the Heroes tab.
*   Fifteen garden achievements, listed on the Heroes tab.
*   The Kin Inspector shows a line of what the Bramblekin is saying.

**Explore**
*   The Wolf Spider chases a moving explorer; caught, the explorer is knocked flat for a moment and drops its pack (never hurt). Sprint to get away.
*   Clanmates ask for berries or water bottles; bring them for thanks.
*   Things to find: the ant hill, a shrine, a village and the Wolf Spider.
*   A Talk button to hear what a nearby Bramblekin is thinking.

**The garden**
*   Planting Day each spring for every farming clan.
*   Puddles after storms, falling leaves in autumn, and a dimmer view in a storm.
*   New-garden scenarios: Drought year, Twins, Lone survivor.

**Settings and saving**
*   Low detail (no grass, flowers or small stones) and Sleep mode (15 fps, dim) for slower phones and long waits.
*   The garden is saved when the app is paused, flushed to disk, with a backup of the previous save; a save from another version is set aside rather than lost.
