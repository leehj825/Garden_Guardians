# Google Play listing text

**App name** (30 characters at most): `Bramblekin: Tiny Kingdoms` (25). The wanted "Bramblekin: Tiny Garden Kingdoms" is 32 characters, two too many for the app name field, so
use `Bramblekin` alone as the name and put "Tiny Garden Kingdoms" in the short description and the feature graphic (which already says it).

**Short description** (80 at most):
`Watch tiny Bramblekin clans grow into villages and kingdoms in a living garden.` (79)

**Full description** (4000 at most):

> Bramblekin: Tiny Garden Kingdoms is a small world that lives on its own.
>
> In a garden full of grass, water and giant trees, tiny Bramblekin are born, grow up, find food and drink, build homes and raise families.
> Clans join into villages, villages ally into kingdoms, and everything that happens is the kin's own doing: hunters, fishers, guards,
> headmen and kings, hard winters, sickness, raiders and the thief ants of the ant hill.
>
> Watch the whole garden from above, tap any Bramblekin or home to see who they are, or step down into the grass and explore on foot.
> Walk, run, pick up food and materials, and look through your item bag while the kin eat and drink on their own.
>
> - A living simulation: nobody is scripted, every garden turns out differently
> - Villages, kingdoms, borders, alliances and wars
> - Seasons and weather, food and water, a day and a night
> - Explore mode: walk and run through the garden, pick things up
> - Plays upright or sideways; three save slots
> - Free, with a banner ad

**Category:** Simulation. **Contains ads:** yes.

**Package name:** `com.bramblekin.game` (cannot be changed once uploaded).
